    Every way to use it

    Screenshots from ClickUp with the extension installed. The buttons look like ClickUp's own and follow its light and dark theme.

    Copy one task

    List rows

    Hover a row and click the link button to copy that task. The button next to it adds the task to your list. Both sit right after ClickUp's own row buttons.

    Board cards

    The same two buttons appear in the corner of a card when you hover it. Cards already in your list get a purple outline.

    Subtasks

    Subtasks on an open task get the buttons too, next to ClickUp's add and rename buttons.

    Open task

    Copy link and Add to list sit in the task header. The copied title uses the ID shown there, so custom IDs like APP-451 come through.

    Collect a few, copy them together

    Hold ⌥ and click

    Hold ⌥ and click any task: a row, a card, a subtask, or a task link in a doc or comment. A dashed outline shows what will be added. Tasks in your list turn purple; click again to take one out.

    The dock

    Once your list has a task, a bar appears at the bottom of ClickUp. Copy N links copies everything, and so does ⌥⇧C from anywhere in ClickUp. Removed or cleared something by mistake? The message that follows has an Undo button.

    Preview before you paste

    Click the count to see exactly what will paste. Drag rows to reorder them, copy or remove a single task, and turn task IDs on or off.

    From the toolbar

    Popup and paste settings

    The toolbar button opens the same list, plus settings: the task ID prefix, the plain-text format for code editors and terminals, and clearing the list after copying. It works on ClickUp pages only.

    Reads right wherever you paste

    Each copy carries a rich version and a plain one, and every app picks what it understands.

    Slack, Notion, Google Docs, Gmail
